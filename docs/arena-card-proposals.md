# Proposed cards for the new status effects

Status: **proposals for review.** None of these cards exist yet. Each one uses an effect from
`docs/status-effects-rules.md`, and all of them can be tried in the Test Arena now: use **Load onto next Hit** or
**Put on now** in the console to fake the card's effect during a real match. Names, numbers and flavour are first
drafts; per the project rule, each card's final copy is written one card at a time once approved.

Format: **Name** · type · zone · Market cost · key numbers. Rules text, then flavour in italics.
Market costs follow the current catalogue (common Attacks 2–4 Focus, Weapons 4–6, Gear 3–5).

---

## Scorch (Burn)
1. **Hot Coffee Spill** · Kata · cost 2 · Focus value 1
   Your next Attack this turn, if it Hits, Scorches the target ×1.
   *"Careful, it's hot." It was not careful.*
2. **Toaster Uppercut** · Attack · High · cost 4 · Power 4
   If this Hits, Scorch the target ×1. If they were already Scorched, Scorch ×2 instead.
   *Golden brown on both sides.*
3. **Burn After Reading** · Reaction Item · cost 3
   When an opponent's Attack is Blocked by you, Scorch the attacker ×1.
   *The memo is gone. So is your eyebrow.*

## Ink Blot (Poison)
4. **Leaky Fountain Pen** · Weapon · Hands 1 · cost 5 · +1 Attack Power on your first Attack each turn
   When an Attack with this Hits, Ink Blot the target ×1.
   *Signed, sealed, smudged.*
5. **Toner Cloud** · Consumable · cost 2 · play in your Yell
   Ink Blot the opponent ×2. You lose 1 HP.
   *Do not inhale. Do not ask.*
6. **Correction Fluid** · Consumable · cost 2 · play any time on your turn
   Remove all Ink Blot from yourself and recover 1 HP.
   *Mistakes were made. Mistakes were covered.*

## Laminated (Freeze)
7. **Laminating Press** · Kata · cost 4 · Focus value 1
   Your next Attack this turn, if it Hits, Laminates the target.
   *Permanent record.*
8. **Cold Storage Kick** · Attack · Low · cost 5 · Power 3
   If this Hits a Wet Floor target, Laminate them.
   *Slip, then seal.*
9. **Laminate Ripper** · Attack · Mid · cost 3 · Power 3
   +2 Attack Power against a Laminated target (on top of the tear).
   *Peel here.*

## Stapled (Pinned)
10. **Industrial Stapler** · Gear · cost 4
    Exhaust: after you Block an Attack, Staple the attacker.
    *Holds up to 200 sheets and one martial artist.*
11. **Pinning Stance** · Defense · Any · cost 3 · Guard 2
    If this Blocks, Staple the attacker.
    *Stay right there.*

## Red Tape
12. **Form 27-B** · Consumable · cost 2 · play in your Yell
    Red Tape the opponent ×2.
    *Please allow 6–8 weeks.*
13. **Bureaucratic Wall** · Defense Equipment · Hands 1 · cost 4 · +1 DEF
    Whenever an opponent buys a Market card, Red Tape them ×1 (once per round).
    *Every purchase requires a signature. Several.*

## Denied
14. **Rubber Stamp of Refusal** · Reaction Item · cost 4
    When an opponent declares an Attack against you, Deny them (their next card is void).
    *No.*
15. **Appeal Rejected** · Attack · High · cost 4 · Power 3
    If this Hits a fighter who Blocked you this round, Deny them.
    *Your objection has been noted and shredded.*

## Paper Cut (Bleed)
16. **Sharp Memo** · Attack · Mid · cost 2 · Power 2
    If this Hits, Paper Cut the target ×1.
    *Fresh off the printer.*
17. **Envelope Edge** · Weapon · Hands 1 · cost 4
    Your Attacks that Hit Paper Cut the target ×1 (once per turn).
    *Lick it, and regret it.*
18. **First-Aid Tape** · Consumable · cost 1
    Remove 1 Paper Cut from yourself and draw a card.
    *Department-issued. Beige.*

## Wet Floor
19. **Mop Bucket** · Gear · cost 3
    Ascend: Wet Floor an opponent.
    *Caution sign sold separately.*
20. **Slip Sweep** · Attack · Low · cost 3 · Power 2
    +2 Attack Power against a Wet Floor target. If this Hits, they lose Tempo.
    *Physics did most of the work.*

## Barrier (Guarded)
21. **File in Triplicate** · Defense · Any · cost 3 · Guard 1
    If this Blocks, gain Barrier ×2.
    *One for you, one for them, one for the cabinet.*
22. **Accordion Folder** · Defense Equipment · Hands 1 · cost 5 · +1 DEF
    At the start of your turn, gain Barrier ×1 (up to 3).
    *Expands under pressure.*

## Static Shock
23. **Carpet Shuffle** · Kata · cost 2 · Focus value 1
    Your next Attack this turn, if it Hits, Shocks the target.
    *Socks, wool carpet, intent.*
24. **Photocopier Jam** · Attack · Mid · cost 4 · Power 3
    If this Hits a target with Tempo, Shock them and they lose their Tempo.
    *PC LOAD LETTER.*

## Rush Order (Haste)
25. **Express Courier** · Consumable · cost 3 · play in your Ascend
    Gain Rush Order.
    *Absolutely, positively, by Honor.*
26. **Overnight Delivery Kick** · Attack · High · cost 4 · Power 3
    If you have Rush Order, this gets +1 Attack Power and draws you a card if it Hits.
    *Signed for on arrival.*

## Dog-Eared (Brittle)
27. **Crumple Zone** · Attack · Low · cost 3 · Power 3
    If this Hits, Dog-Ear the target ×1.
    *Soft paper folds first.*
28. **Paper Shredder** · Attack · Mid · cost 6 · Power 4
    +1 Attack Power for each stack of Dog-Eared on the target. If this Hits a Dog-Eared target, Paper Cut them ×1.
    *Cross-cut, for your safety.*

## Reimburse (Lifesteal)
29. **Petty Cash Letter Opener** · Weapon · Hands 1 · cost 4
    **Reimburse.** *(When an Attack you make Hits for 2 or more, recover 1 HP. Once per round.)*
    *Every cut is an expense. Every expense is reimbursed.*
30. **Expense Report Buckler** · Defense Equipment · Hands 1 · cost 4 · +1 DEF
    After you Block an Attack, recover 1 HP (once per round).
    *Itemised. Approved. Grudgingly.*

## Combos that tie effects together
31. **Slip and Seal** (Combo, learn 5): *Wet Floor the target, then Hit them with a Low Attack.* Laminate them.
32. **Inkfire** (Combo, learn 5): *Hit a target that has Ink Blot and Scorch.* Both effects tick now, as if it were Honor.
33. **Red Tape Bonfire** (Combo, learn 4): *Hit a target with Red Tape.* Remove their Red Tape; Scorch them ×1 for each stack removed.
34. **Paper Trail** (Combo, learn 4): *Play two Attacks this turn that both Hit.* Paper Cut the target ×2.

## Locations that change effects
35. **Server Room** (Location): Static Shock lasts 1 extra turn. Laminate cannot be torn by Hits of 2 or less.
36. **Break Room Sink** (Location): at Honor, every Scorched fighter loses one stack of Scorch; Wet Floor lasts 1 extra round.
37. **Archive Basement** (Location): Ink Blot fades only every other Honor.

## Suggested first batch to make real
For the best spread with the fewest cards: **Hot Coffee Spill** (Scorch), **Leaky Fountain Pen** (Ink), **Laminating Press** (Laminate),
**Rubber Stamp of Refusal** (Denied), **File in Triplicate** (Barrier), **Petty Cash Letter Opener** (Reimburse), **Slip Sweep** (Wet Floor),
**Crumple Zone** (Dog-Eared). That is two Weapons, two Katas, one Reaction, one Defense and two Attacks, all able to enter booster series.
