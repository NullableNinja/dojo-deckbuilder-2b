# Response to the v1.7 card, rules and balance audit

The audit (an outside review of cards, rules and balance) was run against an **older checkout**. Its design recommendations
mostly still apply, because the cards it names exist with the same text and costs, but several of its facts do not match this
repository. This note records what was checked, what was adopted, and what was changed. The build list that results is
`docs/arena-card-proposals.md`.

## 1. Fact check against this repository (v2.3)

| Audit says | This repository | Effect on the recommendations |
|---|---|---|
| Catalog v1.6 "Economy Draft", 413 entries | **v2.3 "definitive Core catalog", 597 entries** | Counts below were recomputed. |
| IDs like `DDB-COR-KAT-005` | IDs are `DDB-<TYPE>-CORE-NNN` (Clean Slate Drill is `DDB-KAT-CORE-010`) | Every retune was re-mapped **by name**; all 26 cards exist with the audited text and cost. |

## 2. Adopted
- **The balance guardrails**, now section 6 of `docs/status-effects-rules.md`.
- **Most of the revised card texts**: the cycling riders on Hot Coffee Spill, Burn After Reading, Carpet Shuffle and Crumple Zone; the Weapon Attack Bonuses; the once-per-round limits; Accordion Folder capped at 2 Barrier; Paper Shredder capped at +2; Correction Fluid at 2 HP; First-Aid Tape at cost 2.
- **The Combo payoffs**, which consume part of their setup.
- **The three Locations**, rewritten in the v2.3 Location format.
- **The 12 support cards**, built last and only if needed.
- **The 14 draw/thin retunes and 12 Fortress retunes**, as proposals mapped to real IDs.
- **The tag split** (`Deck Control` → `Draw`, `Cycle`, `Deck-Thin`, `Tutor`, `Recursion`).
- **The test scenarios** and the 45–55% win-rate band.

## 3. Arena rule changes made now (guardrails applied to the draft effects)
| Effect | Before | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Laminated | No Attacks **and no Defense cards**, −2 Speed | No Attacks, −2 Speed; Defense cards allowed | One lane per status (guardrail 4) |
| Barrier | Up to 5 stacks | Up to **3** | Not a second Armor layer (guardrail 6) |
| Rush Order | An extra card at **every** Initiate for 2 rounds | One extra card at your **next** Initiate (+2 Speed for 2 rounds) | A cost-3 card gives at most one net card plus a combat bonus (guardrail 1) |

These are in `content/experimental/status-effects.json` and `engine/arena-rules.mjs`, with updated tests in `tests/arena-rules.test.mjs`.

## 4. Kept our version instead of the audit's
- **The status glossary.** The audit proposed lighter definitions; we kept the approved, built ones, with the guardrails applied:
  - Laminated would only stop Tempo and give −1 damage.
  - Rush Order would be +1 damage on one Attack.
  - Wet Floor would affect only the next Low Attack.
  - Denied would be discard-or-cancel.
  - Dog-Eared would be consumed per Attack.

  The card texts are written against our glossary.
- **Laminating Press stays at cost 4** (audit: 3), because our Laminate is the stronger version.
- **Laminate Ripper is +1 Attack Power** (audit: +2), because the tear already adds +2.
- **Rubber Stamp of Refusal is destroyed when used.** The audit's version could trigger on every Attack, every turn.
- **Mop Bucket is a cost-2 Consumable.** As Gear that readies every Initiate, it would keep an opponent permanently on Wet Floor (−2 Speed, −1 DEF).
- **Herbal Tea destroys the Junk it discards.** The audit's "gain 1 Focus" made it a second copy of its own Green Tea.

## 5. Owner decisions (Nov 28)
1. **Market access: no change.** Market Scramble is already an optional house rule; on or off is a matter of taste (decision 92).
2. **Denied: both.** An Attack may be kept by discarding another card, or cancelled; any other card is void (decision 91).
3. **Everything ships now as switchable experimental updates** (decision 90): rules, new cards and retunes each have a module, and
   single cards can be switched off, so each can be tested and then promoted or dropped.
